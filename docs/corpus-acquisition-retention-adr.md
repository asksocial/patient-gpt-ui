# ADR — Corpus Acquisition retention boundaries

**Status — Recommended, pending approval.** Reviewed retention report `30203ef` against foundation `1056b7d` and Gate 2A. The [connector handoff](corpus-acquisition-gate-2a-handoff.md) specifies the finalized integration points and the additional release prerequisites. Provider deletion and policy enforcement remain gated on the approvals below.

## 1. Expire revisions individually

The foundation already stores raw text and its deadline separately from revision identity. `corpus_builder_withdraw(..., 'expired')` currently withdraws the entire post once any revision expires. Use a new bounded `expire_revisions(workspace, cutoff, limit)` operation for scheduled expiry, and preserve record-wide withdrawal for takedowns. Retire the legacy record-wide expiry action and update its callers in the same migration and application release.

- Keep stable workspace-scoped source and revision IDs, foreign keys, frame membership, and frozen manifests. Preserve the existing full-frame dependency of `corpus_builder_version_status`. An expired revision makes its frame and dependent versions unavailable for evidence use, even when the selected subset omitted that post. Historical references still resolve to status and provenance. Versions built from unaffected frames stay valid. A new freeze can use an eligible revision under the existing selection rules.
- Persist each revision’s effective deadline and policy version. Keep the existing collection-time clock and earliest-deadline rule for identical content unless policy owners approve a change. In one transaction under the workspace mutation lock, remove expired text and the URL and author details for that revision’s observations, write a revision-keyed tombstone and invalidation, and append a minimal audit event. Preserve the existing refusal to rehydrate expired content, including after a retry or repeat observation.
- Require every content-bearing read to check workspace, revision availability, and the deadline at query time. Existing frame checks already gate on text presence and expiry. Future embeddings, extracts, caches, exports, and assessments need explicit workspace and revision lineage. Deny access immediately when a dependency expires, then purge or rebuild via a durable, idempotent cleanup queue. Frozen citations resolve to an unavailable revision. Provider data and stale cache entries cannot serve as fallback sources.

## 2. Keep the integrity chain and operational logs separate

`corpus_builder.audit_events` is an append-only workspace chain. Its digest commits the actor, action, resource, timestamp, sequence, and previous digest. The writer advances from the last event. Keep existing rows, digest format, sequencing, and immutability triggers intact. Apply the 90-day proposal to a separate operational store for sanitized diagnostic and delivery metadata, with explicit `expires_at` from event time and bounded purge. Keep content, tokens, and raw provider or callback payloads out of both stores.

Treat replay keys, page checkpoints, run bindings, unresolved retry or deletion state, billing reservations and settlement, approvals, and tombstones as control records. Keep them through their applicable recovery, dispute, or enforcement periods even when diagnostics age out. Callback replay keys can expire only after late or duplicate delivery can no longer cause a new effect. The audit chain, any actor identifiers inside it, and control records retained beyond 90 days require a separately approved schedule. A finite chain retention requirement would need a separate decision for archived verification, trusted anchors, and sequence continuity before any pruning.

## 3. Pin normalization and release datasets explicitly

- At provider fetch and normalization, project only approved fields and pass the canonical normalized rows to `corpus_builder_page`. Gate 2A already pins the mapping digest. Include the field policy, canonicalization version, and any pseudonym key version in that binding, and verify the shape at the database boundary. Preserve `(workspace, attempt, offset)`, row order and count, dataset binding, and stable collection time. The foundation hashes the entire normalized page, so keep the historical projection and hash contract for existing attempts while approved. For a policy change that forbids the old shape, hold the attempt for approved settlement or a new attempt. Preserve old checkpoints. A page newly arriving after its content deadline fails closed under the current contract. A replay of an already committed page can acknowledge its existing checkpoint without restoring content.
- Add a private dataset registry keyed by `(provider, verified account or project scope, immutable dataset ID)`, with workspace-scoped consumer references and a durable deletion intent. Register at acquisition binding. A separate authorized worker claims a deletion intent only after provider execution and settlement are final, ingestion is complete or explicitly abandoned, all consumers have released, and no recovery, reconciliation, in-flight reader, or legal hold remains. `review` can resume and remains a blocker. Where external consumers cannot be enumerated, require exclusive dataset ownership before enabling deletion.
- Serialize the release decision against new binds, resumes, and reads at dataset scope across all associated workspaces. Persist intent before the external call, block new use, and reconcile interrupted deletion under a fenced worker lease. Confirmed deletion or authoritative absence in the verified scope completes the same intent. Authentication failures, ambiguous absence, and unknown outcomes remain pending or in review. Keep dataset deletion separate from run cancellation and from disposal of run inputs or other provider stores used for reconciliation. [Apify dataset deletion API](https://docs.apify.com/api/v2/dataset-delete)

## Migration and retention-branch integration

Sequence additive migrations after foundation `202611180003` and finalized connector `202611180004`. Introduce revision tombstones and access checks first, with revision indexes for observations and frame entries. Inventory existing rows and preserve stored deadlines during any authorized backfill. Then add the operational expiry store and dataset release state. Keep cleanup RPCs capability-scoped and follow existing private-schema ACLs and workspace checks. Integrate at the foundation’s page, withdrawal and frame/status functions, and at the connector’s mapping registration, `bind`, `claim`/`resume`, and final settlement. Leave Evidence Integrity, PV, and hosted migration ledgers outside this change.

## Required regression tests and approvals

- Test mixed-age revisions, the exact expiry boundary before cleanup, shortened deadlines, expired and withdrawn retries, unaffected frames, stable manifests, derived access and purge, and races with ingestion and frame freeze. Verify cross-workspace denial and least-privilege worker access.
- Age operational data past 90 days, purge it, and verify every chain digest and next sequence. Cover late callback replay, open reservations, and unresolved deletion intents. Test old and new mappings, changed payload rejection, lost page acknowledgments, and unchanged row order and counts.
- Use a fake provider for shared datasets, blocked `review`/reconciliation and active reads, competing claim/bind/resume, duplicate release, interrupted deletes, verified absence, and wrong-scope or denied deletion. Replay the additive migrations on a disposable Supabase-compatible profile and rerun the foundation, connector, Evidence Integrity, and PV boundary tests.
- Obtain Legal/Privacy approval for X and Actor collection and reuse rights, permitted fields and purposes, raw durations and clock origins, retroactivity, pseudonyms/native IDs/digests, audit and financial schedules, holds, backups and restored copies, and data-subject requests. Obtain provider and security approval for account ownership, regions and transfers, retention and deletion guarantees, external consumers, and least-privilege deletion authority. The branch’s proposed 30-day raw window remains provisional.
